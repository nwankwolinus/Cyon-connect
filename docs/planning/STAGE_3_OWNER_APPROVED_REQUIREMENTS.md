## 2. S3D-01 — Processing purposes and exclusions (OWNER APPROVED)

Permitted purposes are application administration; applicant identification; membership assessment by authorised parish personnel; applicant communication; duplicate screening; application history, including status, submission history, decisions, rejection and resubmission; and security and accountability.

Emergency-contact information has the separate purpose of contacting the nominated person when an emergency involving the applicant justifies it.

Without separate approval, exclude marketing and commercial promotions, unrelated fundraising, unauthorised distribution to parish members or third parties, general profiling, sharing with other parishes merely because they belong to the same Archdiocese, and unrelated research or analytics.

## 5. S3D-03 — Applicant-information access roles (OWNER APPROVED)

- **President:** full applicant-information access; oversight and final authority for escalated or disputed cases.
- **Secretary:** full applicant-information access; routine application review and administration.
- **PRO:** restricted communication access only, limited to necessary applicant contact information. No complete application record or sacramental/sensitive information; no review, approval, or rejection authority; cannot modify submitted applications or outcomes; cannot grant access to other users.
- **Parish Priest:** authorises access grants and revocations.
- **Technical Administrator:** implements and maintains permissions; receives no applicant-record access automatically and cannot self-grant access.

All grants must be explicit, auditable, and parish-scoped. Revoke access when the relevant authority ends.

## 6. S3D-04 — Data-access boundaries and protection (OWNER APPROVED)

President and Secretary have full applicant-information access within their authorised parish scope. PRO access is restricted to communication needs; restricted fields must not be returned through PRO APIs. Enforce default-deny authorisation, explicit grants and parish scope. Audit sensitive-record access and permission changes without recording sensitive values. Disallow unrestricted bulk exports. Technical administrators have no automatic applicant-data access. Expired sessions and revoked permissions must prevent protected access. Sensitive information must not appear in URLs, logs, or error messages.

## 7. S3D-04A — Date-of-birth confidentiality (OWNER APPROVED)

No parish authority may view an applicant's date of birth (DOB), including President, Secretary, PRO, Parish Priest, and Technical Administrator. DOB is processed only by a restricted server-side eligibility service. It must not appear in reviewer APIs, administrative interfaces, logs, or audit events. Encrypt DOB at rest using separate managed key protection; restrict decryption to the eligibility service; separate DOB from ordinary application and membership records; and do not grant technical administrators unrestricted decryption access. The eligibility service may process DOB in restricted server-side memory; encryption does not prevent that service-side processing access.

## 8. S3D-05 — Parish-confirmed age eligibility (OWNER APPROVED)

Applicants below 16 cannot register. Applicants aged 16 through 40 inclusive are eligible. Applicants above 40 cannot register. Calculate age on the relevant submission date using exact birthday comparison, not simple subtraction of calendar years. An ineligible applicant must not have an application accepted. Parish authorities must not be exposed to DOB.

This decision supersedes the earlier MLD-16 position that no arbitrary age limit should be applied without an approved parish policy. It does not change other MLD-16 field decisions. The implementation must establish eligibility before creating an Auth identity so an applicant below 16 cannot register; the eligibility check must use the restricted service and must not expose DOB to parish authorities. No bypass is permitted. The exact pre-registration interaction and handling of DOB input remain technical design details subject to the approved privacy and security controls.

## 9. S3D-05B — Minors' consent (OWNER APPROVED)

Applicants aged 16–17 must provide parent or legal guardian consent before their application is accepted. Implement the consent method established by the cleared professional assessment. Retain only the minimum evidence needed to demonstrate consent; record timestamp and method; apply restricted access and the approved retention schedule. Do not provide a bypass. The specific consent method and retention period were not available in the repository; do not substitute an invented method or duration.

## 10. S3D-05C — DOB encryption design (OWNER APPROVED)

Use envelope encryption: a data-encryption key is protected by managed KMS, and key material remains outside the application database. Decryption is restricted to the eligibility service. Exclude DOB from logs, URLs, reviewer APIs, and audit events. Provide key rotation and recovery; prohibit unrestricted technical-administrator decryption; complete a security assessment before deployment.

## 11. S3D-06 / S3D-06A–06E — Retention and disposal (OWNER APPROVED)

### S3D-06A — Principles

Apply purpose limitation and data minimisation; define separate retention periods by data category; securely delete or irreversibly anonymise where appropriate; and make deletion/archival auditable without retaining sensitive payloads.

### S3D-06B — Application lifecycle

- **Approved applications:** preserve relevant history and link it to the member record; do not assume every application field is retained indefinitely.
- **Rejected applications:** preserve the rejection decision and relevant history for the approved limited period.
- **Resubmissions:** create a new application linked to the prior rejected application; never overwrite history.
- **Pending applications:** send reminders and eventually administratively close under the approved inactivity policy. Inactivity alone is not rejection.
- **Unsubmitted drafts:** retain for 30 days after the applicant's last successful draft save/activity. Each successful save resets `last_activity_at` and the 30-day expiry. Expiry is calculated server-side; expired drafts cannot be resumed and their saved applicant payload must be securely deleted on a reliable scheduled basis. No DOB or DOB-derived value may be persisted in a draft. Applicants re-enter DOB when resuming; eligibility is checked server-side on submission.
- **Withdrawn applications:** support withdrawal, restrict further processing, and apply the approved retention/disposal schedule.

### S3D-06C — Emergency-contact data

Retain emergency-contact data only while its legitimate emergency-contact purpose exists. Do not automatically transfer it to a permanent member record. Permit the applicant to correct or replace the nominee. Securely delete it when its purpose ends unless separate justified retention applies. Restrict access.

### S3D-06D — DOB and guardian-consent evidence

Retain DOB only for the approved eligibility purpose and do not automatically transfer it into the permanent member record. Retain the minimum guardian-consent evidence needed to demonstrate valid consent for applicants aged 16–17, and securely delete it when the approved purpose expires.

### S3D-06E — Audit

Use a professionally approved audit-retention schedule. Restrict audit access to explicitly designated authorised audit personnel. Exclude DOB, sacramental details, passwords, tokens, and unnecessary PII from audit records. Record sensitive-record access, permission grants/revocations, administrative actions, and archival/disposal actions. Protect audit records from unauthorised alteration or deletion. Keep security audit separate from membership review history.

Other category-specific retention periods, including rejected applications and security audit, must be recovered from the cleared professional assessment and its approved schedule. The 30-day unsubmitted-draft period above is explicitly owner-approved. Do not invent durations or substitute arbitrary values for other categories.

## 12. S3D-07 / S3D-07A–07D — Duplicate screening (OWNER APPROVED)

### S3D-07A — Approach and process

A possible duplicate is a reason to investigate, not a reason for automatic rejection. Screening criteria are full name, DOB (restricted to server-side comparison), phone number, verified email, and previous/current parish as supporting evidence. The system flags a possible match; the application is flagged for investigation; the Secretary investigates and records findings; unresolved or disputed cases escalate to the President; an authorised reviewer records resolution; screening evidence and resolution history are preserved. No automatic rejection, member merge, or modification of existing member records is permitted.

### S3D-07B — Permitted evidence sources

Permitted sources are existing parish membership records, CYON Connect application history, applicant-provided information, and additional evidence voluntarily supplied by the applicant when clarification is necessary. External databases, other parishes' records, and unofficial third-party lists are excluded without separate authorisation and appropriate legal basis.

### S3D-07C — Matching rules

An exact verified-email match, exact phone-number match, or exact DOB match flags investigation; DOB comparison is server-side only and DOB is never disclosed to reviewers. Name similarity and previous/current parish are supporting evidence only; name similarity alone is never sufficient. Multiple indicators increase investigation priority but do not automatically establish duplicate identity.

### S3D-07D — Outcomes

1. **Confirmed duplicate:** block approval and member creation until properly resolved; never merge automatically.
2. **Not a duplicate:** remove the screening hold and permit normal processing.
3. **Inconclusive:** remain unresolved; escalate disputed cases to the President; no approval or member creation while unresolved.

Each outcome records reviewer, decision date, rationale, and relevant evidence references. Do not copy sensitive information into security audit logs.

## 13. S3D-08 — Application statuses and lifecycle (OWNER APPROVED)

Approved application statuses are **Draft, Submitted, Under Review, On Hold, Approved, Rejected, Withdrawn, Closed**. Submission does not mean approval or membership. Applicants may edit only before review begins. Rejected applications cannot be reopened through editing. Resubmission creates a new linked application. Unresolved duplicate screening blocks approval. Inactivity alone is not rejection. Every status transition must be authorised and recorded in application history.

The implementation must preserve these exact statuses and rules. Any further transition guards or concurrency representation must implement, not redefine, the approved lifecycle.

## 14. S3D-09 — Applicant notifications (OWNER APPROVED)

Send notifications to the verified email for application submission, placement on hold, clarification request, approval, rejection, withdrawal, and administrative closure. Emails must contain no sensitive application information, internal screening evidence, or reviewer discussions. Record delivery attempts and failures. Email delivery failure must never change application status.

## 15. S3D-10 — Submission integrity (OWNER APPROVED)

Every submission attempt carries a unique idempotency key. Repeating the same key with an identical payload returns the original result and reference number. Reusing the key with a different payload returns a conflict and cannot overwrite the original application. Application creation, initial status, submission history, and required audit event must succeed or fail together in one database transaction. Issue a successful submission receipt only after database commit. Failed or uncertain requests must be safely retryable without creating duplicate applications. A legitimate resubmission after rejection creates a new linked application.

## 16. Cross-cutting implementation and status rules

1. Stage 3 submission persists an application only; it does not create a member, create or associate `user_accounts`, approve membership, activate an account, or grant reviewer permissions.
2. Applicant ownership comes from server-verified Supabase Auth identity. Never trust caller-supplied applicant IDs. Parish assignment comes from trusted server-side configuration resolving the single operational parish, SS Joachim and Anne Catholic Church, Ijegun.
3. Reviewer access uses explicit, auditable grants and parish scope, with default deny. Secretary/President/PRO/Priest/technical-administrator powers are bounded by §§5–6. `PLATFORM_ADMIN` grants no automatic membership authority or applicant-record access.
4. Application history and security audit are distinct. Transaction-bound required audit writes commit atomically with successful business writes. Notifications are outside the transaction; their failure cannot change application status.
5. This baseline does not establish general supporting-document requirements. A later explicit owner-approved passport-photograph requirement is recorded in §19; it is the sole currently approved document/image requirement and does not authorize other uploads.
6. Do not infer that approved policy or a design requirement is implemented. No code, schema, encryption/KMS control, access path, retention job, notification service, or professional assessment is evidenced by this document.

## 19. Owner-approved requirement — mandatory passport photograph

The owner has explicitly approved the passport photograph as mandatory before final application submission. This is an operative requirement, not a recommendation or an inference from `FR-MEM-006`.

- A draft may be saved without a photograph. The form identifies the photograph as required for submission, previews a successfully uploaded image, and permits replacement/removal while the application remains a draft.
- The server must validate actual image content, applicant ownership, draft association, accessibility and verification immediately before submission. The database submission transaction must atomically attach that verified photograph to the application. Missing, invalid, inaccessible, mismatched, unverified or caller-forged references must fail closed.
- Draft photographs remain subject to the approved 30-day inactivity period. Submitted-photograph retention is a separate unresolved schedule; 30 days must not be assumed for submitted photographs.
- Store images only in a private Supabase Storage bucket. No public bucket, public URL, direct browser storage access, member creation, or account association is authorized by this requirement.
- Technical implementation choice: accept JPEG, PNG and WebP uploads up to 5 MiB; decode the image server-side, require at least 300 × 300 pixels and no more than 6,000 × 6,000 pixels / 36 megapixels, reject animation/corrupt content, then normalize to JPEG and strip metadata. These are validation limits, not membership eligibility rules.
- After submission, draft operations cannot replace or remove the attached photograph. Preserve it with the submitted application subject to the separately approved retention schedule.

Status distinction: the requirement is **OWNER APPROVED**. Local code and migration work is **IMPLEMENTED LOCALLY** only where traceability evidence states so; automated checks are **VERIFIED LOCALLY** only when executed.
