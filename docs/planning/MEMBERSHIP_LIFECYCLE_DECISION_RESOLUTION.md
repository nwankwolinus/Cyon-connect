## 2. Decision-resolution matrix

### MLD-01 — Application/member separation and creation timing

- **Question / requirements:** Can an application exist before a member, and when may a new member be created? `FR-MEM-001`, `FR-MEM-002`, `FR-MEM-004`, `FR-MEM-005`, `FR-SEC-003`.
- **Verified:** `public.members` is a stable identity record; there is no application entity. A member may exist without an account. The member and account statuses are independent (`20261008090000_identity_foundation.sql`).
- **Options and consequence:** Create a member at submission (conflates application and identity); create only after approval (separates them and avoids placeholder identities); defer member creation indefinitely (cannot establish the approved member record).
- **Recommendation / disposition:** Applications are durable and independent; create new members only after approval. This development-planning decision and the current recommendation were explicitly approved by the Project Owner on 22 October 2026. **`APPROVED — EVIDENCE RECORDED`.** See the [owner approval record](MEMBERSHIP_LIFECYCLE_OWNER_APPROVAL.md), MLD-01.
- **If unresolved:** implementation risks duplicate/placeholder identity records or application loss.

### MLD-02 — Existing-member association

- **Question / requirements:** When and on what basis can an application be associated with a member? `FR-MEM-002`, `FR-MEM-011`, `FR-MEMA-003`, `FR-SEC-008`, `FR-SEC-010`.
- **Verified:** the schema has no person-level matching key or duplicate-resolution workflow. `user_accounts_auth_user_key` and `user_accounts_member_key` constrain account links, not human identity uniqueness.
- **Options and consequence:** Automatically associate from similar attributes (unsafe identity substitution); require authorized review and recorded evidence (reduces false links but requires reviewer process); allow no association (cannot complete verified existing-member cases).
- **Recommendation / disposition:** Existing-member association requires authorized review; similarity alone is insufficient; no merge, relink, or mutation of existing identity records. The Project Owner approved this planning policy on 22 October 2026. **`APPROVED — EVIDENCE RECORDED`.** The data/evidence standard and review dispositions were not specified by the recommendation and remain implementation prerequisites. See owner approval record, MLD-02.
- **If unresolved:** the application can be attached to the wrong person or silently create a duplicate.

### MLD-03 — Rejection, resubmission, and lineage

- **Question / requirements:** How are rejection and a later submission represented? `FR-MEM-004`, `FR-MEM-009`, `FR-MEM-010`, `FR-MEMA-003`, `FR-AUD-001`–`004`.
- **Verified:** membership status has no `rejected` value; no application/history model exists.

Do not impose a one-successor limit absent an approved rule. The Project Owner approved this policy on 22 October 2026. **`APPROVED — EVIDENCE RECORDED`.** Detailed eligibility and branch rules remain implementation details. See owner approval record, MLD-03.
- **If unresolved:** rejected outcomes may be misrepresented as member status or overwritten.

### MLD-04 — Potential-match sequencing and duplicate-review gate

- **Question / requirements:** Must a potential match be resolved before final application approval, and what must precede creation of a new member? Candidate `FR-MEM-002` requires one member record per person subject to authorized duplicate resolution; `FR-MEM-011` requires potential duplicates routed for authorized review; `FR-MEM-004` governs application review.
- **Verified:** neither requirement specifies sequencing, match criteria, evidence, or outcomes. The schema cannot enforce one natural person per `members` row. Foreign keys and unique application/member associations do not prove identity.
- **Options and consequence:** (A) Resolve a potential match before final approval—best avoids approving a case that may create a duplicate, but can delay approval while ambiguous evidence is reviewed. (B) Allow approval while a match is pending but block member creation/association—preserves the approval decision but requires a defined approved-but-unresolved state and communication/recovery behavior. (C) Ignore an unresolved possible match and create a member—risks violating the one-person invariant and is not recommended.
- **Operational recommendation:** Submission creates only a durable application and operation-scoped idempotency record; no member or account is created. A duplicate screen may return (1) no apparent match, which is not proof of uniqueness; (2) possible match, requiring scoped human review; (3) confirmed association, which prevents new-member creation; or (4) insufficient evidence, which remains unresolved. The screen must use only approved applicant/member data and a defined process; no threshold, identifier, or matching algorithm is assumed. A human resolution records reviewer, scope, disposition, concise rationale/evidence reference, and time, minimizing exposed PII. No apparent match permits consideration of a new-member path only after the approved procedure is recorded. Possible or insufficient evidence blocks final approval and creation under the recommended sequencing. A confirmed match associates to the existing member only after authorized review; it never changes `members` or `user_accounts` identity links. The candidate FRS assigns Secretary routine application review and President oversight, but neither the permission matrix nor match-resolution authority is implemented or sufficiently specified; deny until explicit action permission and scope are approved. Similarity, a foreign key, and a unique application/member link do not establish that two records concern different people.
- **Recommendation / disposition:** Option A is approved: resolve a possible match before final application approval; re-check the recorded resolution in the member-creation transaction. The Project Owner approved this policy on 22 October 2026. **`APPROVED — EVIDENCE RECORDED`.** No trustworthy person-level key or screening capability exists in the repository, so exact screening data, evidence recording, permissions, and persisted representation remain technical implementation prerequisites, not grounds to bypass the approved gate.
- **If unresolved:** fail closed: keep the application pending review; do not finalize approval, associate a member, or create one when a possible match or insufficient evidence remains. The database alone cannot guarantee FR-MEM-002 person-level uniqueness.
- **Implementation gate:** the policy recommendation is approved by the Project Owner. Implementation planning may proceed. Do not enable member creation for an application until a privacy-reviewed applicant/member data model, recorded human screening disposition, reviewer permission/scope, and transactionally revalidated gate exist. The database cannot guarantee person-level uniqueness; human evidence and an authorized decision remain part of the control.

### MLD-05 — Match outcomes and identity evidence

- **Question / requirements:** What outcomes may authorized duplicate review record, and what proves a confirmed match? Candidate `FR-MEM-002`, `FR-MEM-011`, `FR-MEM-006`, `FR-MEM-012`, `FR-PRI-003`.
- **Verified:** no matching algorithm, verified unique identifier integration, or identity-verification capability is implemented or specified by the inspected source.
- **Conceptual handling:** “no apparent match” means the approved search/review procedure found no candidate; it is not proof that the person is unique. “Possible match” means the application is held for authorized review, with no automatic link, rejection, or new-member creation. “Confirmed match” is an authorized human determination based on evidence allowed by approved policy; only then may a protected operation record an association. Record actor, decision, rationale/evidence reference, scope, and time while minimizing personal data.
- **Options and consequence:** Similarity score alone (unsafe); human review using approved evidence and rationale (auditable but dependent on a yet-to-be-approved evidence policy); external authoritative verification (requires an approved integration and evidence of availability).
- **Recommendation / disposition:** Human review records reviewer, decision/disposition, rationale or evidence reference, scope, and time while minimizing displayed PII. No particular identifier, threshold, or external service is selected. The Project Owner approved this bounded human-review policy on 22 October 2026. **`APPROVED — EVIDENCE RECORDED`** for that policy direction; implementing a specific screening method remains prohibited until available data and evidence handling are defined and reviewed.
- **If unresolved:** reviewers cannot consistently distinguish a confirmed association from a possible match.
- **Implementation gate:** the owner-approved policy permits a bounded human-review approach without an invented identifier, threshold, or automated matcher. Before implementing any match disposition, define the allowed applicant/member fields, human evidence-reference/rationale handling, permission/scope check, and audit record. If the reviewer cannot establish identity from approved evidence, leave the case unresolved and create/associate no member.

### MLD-06 — Approval and member-creation consistency

- **Question / requirements:** Can approval, member creation/association, history, and required audit commit consistently? `FR-MEM-002`, `FR-MEM-004`, `FR-MEM-010`, `FR-MEMA-004`, `FR-AUD-001`–`004`, `FR-SEC-008`.
- **Verified:** PostgreSQL functions in the identity-provisioning and bootstrap migrations perform their own transactional writes; no membership application operation exists. Nothing establishes cross-system atomicity.
- **Options and consequence:** One database transaction (all-or-nothing for the database writes, simplest recovery); durable multi-transaction workflow (necessary only if a real boundary prevents one transaction, but requires explicit persisted progress, retries, monitoring and reconciliation).
- **Recommendation / disposition:** Use one protected PostgreSQL transaction for current outcome, member creation or association, business review history, and required database audit. Roll back all those writes on validation or audit failure. If a genuine external boundary requires multiple transactions, design a durable, idempotent recovery contract; do not claim cross-transaction atomicity. Do not send notifications or trigger irreversible effects before commit. The Project Owner approved this technical design direction on 22 October 2026. **`APPROVED — EVIDENCE RECORDED`**; implementation must still prove the specified transaction, retry, and recovery behavior.
- **Guarantees to design:** unique operation identity; one successful decision against the current application version; retry returns/reconciles the same result; no duplicate member from retry; committed approval has a visible completion/recovery outcome. Existing account-link uniqueness does not deduplicate a person.
- **If unresolved:** approval can be committed without its member/audit result, or retries can produce duplicate identities.

### MLD-07 — Membership status after approval

- **Question / requirements:** Which member status follows approval, and what happens when an existing member is associated? Candidate `FR-MEM-009`, `FR-MEM-005`, `FR-SEC-004`, `FR-SEC-008`, `FR-SEC-010`.
- **Verified:** membership vocabulary is `pending`, `active`, `inactive`, `suspended`, `archived`; account vocabulary is `pending_activation`, `active`, `suspended`, `deactivated`. No transitions are implemented. Get My Identity requires active account and membership, but that is normal identity retrieval, not lifecycle policy.
- **Options and consequence:** Set a newly created member `active` on approved membership (makes the approved membership effective, but must not itself activate an account); set it `pending` (preserves the database default but may conflate pre-approval with post-approval because no transition meaning is specified); or define another status/phase (requires approved semantics and potentially schema change). No existing transition rule resolves the choice.
- **Recommendation / disposition:** Create a newly approved member with status `active`, meaning approved membership only, in the same member-creation transaction; keep `user_accounts` absent or in its separate non-active activation lifecycle. Normal identity retrieval still requires the separately active account. The migration's `pending` default is not a lifecycle decision, and `FR-MEM-009` supplies vocabulary only. The Project Owner approved this status recommendation and its separation from account activation on 22 October 2026. **`APPROVED — EVIDENCE RECORDED`.** Implementation must gate every access path on the required account activation and must not grant privileges from membership status alone. Until that enforcement is in place, do not expose access or complete a workflow that would grant such privileges.
- **Existing-member behavior:** `active`: after confirmed identity review, associate without changing status. `inactive`, `suspended`, or `archived`: do not reactivate, unsuspend, restore, or otherwise mutate the member; do not complete an approval that depends on changing that status. Hold for a separately authorized membership-status decision. `pending`: likewise do not infer that application approval changes it; hold for an explicit disposition. In every case, never change `user_accounts.member_id` or account status through application association.
- **If unresolved:** status defaults can grant or deny membership privileges contrary to policy; account activation and membership approval may be conflated.
- **Implementation gate:** new-member creation after approval may proceed only after approval of the `active` membership meaning and confirmation of privilege gating while account activation is incomplete. Existing-member association may proceed only for a confirmed active member; policy for `pending`, `inactive`, `suspended`, and `archived` members must be approved before those cases are completed. No account or identity-link mutation is part of this operation.

### MLD-08 — Current application outcome and business review history

- **Question / requirements:** Store current outcome or derive it from events? `FR-MEM-004`, `FR-MEM-010`, `FR-MEMA-004`, `FR-AUD-001`–`004`.
- **Verified:** neither application outcomes nor review history exist. The existing identity audit notes say `updated_at` is not an audit trail.
- **Options and consequence:** Current outcome plus append-only history (simple reads and compare-and-set; requires atomic dual writes); derive outcome from events (single event authority; requires replay/order/version semantics and more complex reads/recovery).
- **Recommendation / disposition:** Keep an authoritative current outcome on the application and append-only business review history. In one transaction, check current version/outcome, update it, and append the attributed event. Stale decisions conflict and write neither a new outcome nor a successful event. Corrections are attributable corrective events, not history rewrites. The Project Owner approved this technical model on 22 October 2026. **`APPROVED — EVIDENCE RECORDED`**; no application model or history implementation exists yet.
- **Lifecycle semantics:** A valid submission begins in a semantic received/awaiting-review condition; no persisted label is selected. Approval and rejection update the application outcome, not membership status. A resubmission is a new application linked to its rejected predecessor and preserves both histories; no one-successor limit is recommended absent an approved rule. Do not impose terminality or other transitions not yet decided.
- **Business history vs security audit:** business history explains submission review, decision, escalation, match resolution, and correction. Security audit records privileged/denied access and administrative security actions. Successful business changes and required audit writes commit together; a rolled-back transaction cannot preserve its own audit insert. Denied events need a separate reliable mechanism where necessary, not claimed as currently implemented.
- **If unresolved:** current state and history can disagree or audit records can be mistaken for business decisions.

Correct an erroneous association only through an authorized, attributable operation retaining the former link, actor, reason, and time; never alter member/account identity links or merge identities. ## 3. Approved policy/design baseline and remaining implementation detail

The owner-approved policy and design directions are:

1. No automatic person matching or association; unresolved possible/insufficient matches cannot pass the approved-before-final-approval gate or proceed to member creation.
2. Authorized human review records a scoped disposition and minimized rationale/evidence reference; no automatic threshold or external identity integration is assumed.
3. New members are created only after approval with status `active` as approved membership; account activation remains separate and gates access as required. Existing non-active members are never implicitly changed.
4. No lifecycle mutation through direct client DML or unguarded service-role access; enforce explicit action permission, actor, current state, and organizational scope server-side.
5. Approval, member/association write, business history, and required audit use one PostgreSQL transaction where they share that boundary; failure rolls back and stale/replayed requests cannot duplicate an outcome.
6. Current application outcome and append-only review history remain consistent; corrections do not rewrite prior history.
7. Secretary/President authority boundaries are as recorded in MLD-11; other executives and `PLATFORM_ADMIN` have no implied membership-review authority.

Implementation details still required before the affected feature ships include persisted application state/event names, the physical representation and access controls for the approved applicant fields, human evidence references and review workflow, exact permission identifiers and scope representation, idempotency storage/retention, member-status enforcement across every access path, and audit retention/denied-event handling. These details must implement the approved baseline and must not reopen it by default.
