## 1. Partial dues payment

- **Why it matters:** determines allocation logic and the dues ledger model (FR-DUE-008) and what a reversal restores (FR-DUE-011).
- **Possible options:** reject partials; accept and hold a part-paid month; accept and apply oldest-first.
- **Needed by:** before Sprint 5 design. **Proposed owner:** Financial Secretary, with Parish Priest/Youth Chaplain and President.

## 2. Dues overpayment

- **Why it matters:** an overpayment can become advance months, a credit balance or a refund; each has different ledger and audit consequences.
- **Possible options:** auto-apply to future months; hold as credit; reject excess.
- **Needed by:** before Sprint 5 design. **Proposed owner:** Financial Secretary, with Chaplain/Treasurer.

## 3. Duplicate-member merge and identity repair (application screening policy recorded separately)

- **Why it matters:** the approved membership-application policy requires possible matches to be resolved by authorized review before final approval, but it does not authorize merging or repairing identity records. Merge could affect attendance, dues and history (`FR-MEM-010`, `FR-MEMA-005`) and the one-person/one-account rule.
- **Possible options:** keep merge/identity repair out of scope; define a separately authorized, audited correction procedure; or maintain a proposed relationship without merging identities. No option is selected here.
- **Needed by:** before any merge, relink, or identity-repair feature. This open item does not block implementing the approved application screening gate without identity mutation.

## 4. Detailed messaging-group governance

- **Why it matters:** who may create groups, administer them and add members affects privacy (FR-MSG-010, FR-SEC-017) and moderation.
- **Possible options:** executive-created official groups only; member-created with limits; staged (FRS §24.2 lists "enhanced messaging governance" as Phase 2).
- **Needed by:** before Sprint 9. **Proposed owner:** President and Secretary, with Parish Priest/Youth Chaplain.

## Project-owner approvals — Sprint 1B.2C (application use-case scope)

Recorded 2026-10-12 from the project owner's approval: FRS v1.0 and Stakeholder Requirements v1.0 are the **working development baseline**, subject to later formal sign-off. This approval does not represent formal requirements sign-off.

For the read-only **Get My Identity** application use case only, the approved normal-access policy is:

- Permit retrieval only when the caller's account is `active` and membership is `active`.
- Deny normal identity-context access when the account is `pending_activation`, `suspended`, or `deactivated`.
- Deny normal identity-context access when membership is `pending`, `inactive`, `suspended`, or `archived`, even when the account is active.
- These access restrictions do not delete or deactivate records and do not define recovery procedures.
- Administrative exceptions, recovery, activation, status transitions, relinking, transfers, and duplicate-member resolution remain out of scope.

This is application behavior for this use case. It does not change database RLS, grants, persisted statuses, or security policy. ID-4 remains open for database/RLS semantics and other application operations; ID-3, ID-5, ID-6, ID-7, and the merge/identity-repair portion of membership decision #3 remain open.

### Decisions recorded

1. **Membership review authority:** Owner-approved policy for CYON Connect: the Secretary handles routine application reviews, approvals, and rejections. The President provides oversight and has final decision authority for escalated or disputed cases. Other executives have no review authority by default; actions require explicit permission. `PLATFORM_ADMIN` does not automatically grant membership-review or approval authority. Protected actions require server-side checks of authenticated actor, explicit permission, applicable scope, and current application state. Exact permission identifiers and implementation remain open. Traceability: FR-MEM-004, FR-MEMA-001–003, FR-SEC-002, FR-SEC-008, FR-SEC-010.
2. **Rejection, resubmission, and duplicates:** Rejection is an application outcome distinct from membership status. Resubmission is permitted under rules to be defined and must link to the previously rejected application while preserving both histories. Do not map rejection to `inactive` or add a `rejected` membership status. Potential duplicates are routed for authorized review; they do not automatically create another application or reject an applicant. Duplicate review requires explicit permission and scope; identity conflicts follow the owner-approved President escalation boundary. Traceability: FR-MEM-004, FR-MEM-009, FR-MEMA-003. Merge and identity repair remain open separately.
3. **Approval and activation:** Membership approval starts a separate account-activation workflow and must not automatically activate an account. Traceability: FR-MEM-005, FR-SEC-004.
4. **Activation authority:** A controlled system workflow performs account activation only after required identity and security checks pass. Activation states, verification, retries, expiry, and failure handling remain undefined. Traceability: FR-MEM-005, FR-SEC-004, FR-SEC-008, FR-SEC-010.
5. **Audit policy:** Owner-approved minimum fields are authenticated actor identifier or defined system-actor attribution for automated actions; affected member/application identifier; action; previous and resulting state where applicable; timestamp; outcome/failure category where applicable; and correlation/evidence reference where available. Audit reads must be explicitly authorized, scoped, and read-only. A business-state change and its required audit event must succeed in the same transaction. Security-relevant denied attempts must be recorded without secrets or unnecessary sensitive payloads; where the failed business transaction cannot record a denial, a separate reliable recording mechanism is required. No such mechanism is established or proven here. Traceability: FR-MEMA-004, FR-AUD-001–004, FR-CC-007.
6. **Member creation timing:** Create a new `public.members` identity only after membership approval. A submitted application is a separate durable record and does not require a member record; do not create a member for each submission or resubmission. Traceability: FR-MEM-001, FR-MEM-004, FR-MEM-005, FR-MEM-009.
7. **Existing-member association:** If an application may belong to an existing member, require authorized review before linking it. Similar names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other attributes alone do not authorize automatic association. This decision does not authorize merge, relink, or modification of existing identity records. Traceability: FR-MEM-011, FR-MEMA-003, FR-SEC-008, FR-SEC-010.
